# Watching the Split — open discussion

This repository holds the public discussion for **[Watching the Split](https://marin.kokona.website/philosophy/watching-the-split)**, a set of wiki-style pages on how to live with a reality that holds the one and the not, with the hard problem of consciousness as the worked example. It follows on from [Natural Process](https://marin.kokona.website/philosophy/natural-process) and [Folding](https://marin.kokona.website/philosophy/folding).

There is no code here. The conversation lives in **[Discussions](https://github.com/marink/watching-the-split/discussions)**.

## How to take part

- **Objections** — your sharpest one is the most welcome. Post it under **Objections**, and say which page it is about. A good objection that sits here in the open, beside the text, under your name, is the point of this space.
- **Questions** — under **Q&A**.
- **Elsewhere in the graph** — a node, a link or a tradition the pages leave out, under **Elsewhere in the graph**.

Readers are encouraged to answer one another. You do not need the author's permission to disagree with him, or with each other.

## What to expect

The author reads what arrives. Replies are not promised. These pages were published this way, rather than through peer review, to invite more scrutiny without a verdict; the discussion is that scrutiny, in public.

Please read the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) before posting.
