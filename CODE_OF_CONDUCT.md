# Code of conduct

This is a space for argument, and argument is welcome at full strength. The rules exist to keep it an argument.

## Do

- **Attack the idea, never the person.** Hand over the sharpest version of your objection; leave the person out of it.
- **Quote or link what you are answering**, so others can check it.
- **Keep it checkable.** Say what would show you wrong, and expect others to ask.
- **Disagree with anyone**, including the author and including each other.

## Do not

- Insult, harass, threaten or demean anyone, for any reason.
- Use slurs or abusive profanity.
- Post spam, advertising, or anything off-topic in bulk.
- Post anyone's private information.
- Mock a person's religion, or lack of one. Criticise frames and claims, not believers or unbelievers.

## Moderation

Comments that break these rules may be hidden or deleted, threads may be locked, and accounts may be blocked from the repository, without warning in clear cases. Moderation is about conduct, not opinion: no objection will be removed for disagreeing.

To report a problem, use **Report content** on the comment, or GitHub's own reporting for abuse and spam.
